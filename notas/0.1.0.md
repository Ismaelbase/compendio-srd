First release.

- Imports the official SRD 5.2.1 into your vault as linked notes, in Spanish, English, German, French or Italian: creatures, spells, magic items, conditions, rules, glossary, classes and subclasses, species, backgrounds, feats, weapons, armor, tools, gear, poisons and traps.
- 2024 stat blocks in plain Markdown, with properties to sort and filter, and index notes for each section.
- Encounter builder with the 2024 XP budget and the troubleshooting tips of the SRD. Sends the encounter to Initiative Tracker, with your Initiative Tracker players, or copies an encounter block.
- Optional Fantasy Statblocks properties, with a 2024 stat block layout for each language.
- Feet and pounds, or the metric units of the SRD in Spanish, German, French and Italian.
- No network access: all the data is inside the plugin.
